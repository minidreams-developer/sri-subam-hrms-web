# Product Overview

This is an HRMS (Human Resource Management System) admin panel for managing employees, staff, teams, branches, attendance, leave management, overtime, shifts, and offboarding processes.

## Core Features

- Employee and staff management with detailed profiles
- Team organization and branch management
- Time tracking (attendance, leave, overtime)
- Shift scheduling and management
- Offboarding workflows
- Dashboard with analytics and quick stats
- Order and delivery management
- Payment tracking and analytics

## User Roles

- Admin users with full access to the system
- Employee and staff records with different permission levels
- Authentication with JWT token-based access control

## Key Modules

- **People Hub**: Employees, teams, branches
- **Time Hub**: Leave attendance, overtime management, shift management
- **Dashboard**: Statistics, revenue analytics, top products
- **Orders & Deliveries**: Order history, delivery tracking, status management
